### Sprint Zero

Sprint Zero is a foundational phase in any SitecoreAI project, setting the stage for a smooth and efficient development process. This critical sprint focuses on establishing the project, ensuring your team has the tools, processes, and baseline structure needed to deliver quality outcomes with minimal friction. Investing time in this phase minimizes risks, streamlines workflows, and lays the groundwork for scalable and maintainable solutions in SitecoreAI.
