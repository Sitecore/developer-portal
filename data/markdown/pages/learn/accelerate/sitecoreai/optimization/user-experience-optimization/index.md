### User Experience Optimization

Optimizing the user experience turns insight into action. Once content, structure, and performance are in place, the next step is to refine how visitors engage with your site. In SitecoreAI that means three connected activities: personalizing content for audiences you understand, testing ideas where you do not know the answer yet, and using visitor data to decide what to do next.

These recipes cover the work in the order most teams need it.
